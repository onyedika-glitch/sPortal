Official Announcements

University Convocation Ceremony 2025

Date: November 22, 2025

The 19th Convocation Ceremony for graduating students will hold at the University Main Auditorium. All graduating students are expected to complete clearance before November 15.

Mid-Semester Examination Schedule

Release Date: October 10, 2025

Students are informed that the Mid-Semester exams will commence on October 21. Departmental timetables will be uploaded to the portal by October 14.

ICT Maintenance Notice

Date: October 15, 2025

The ICT department will carry out maintenance on the student portal from 12 AM – 6 AM. During this time, access to results and registration will be temporarily unavailable.

New Course Registration Deadline

Deadline: October 25, 2025

Students are advised to complete all course registration and approval before the stated date. Late registration will attract an additional fee.

Student Union Election 2025

Date: November 5, 2025

The Independent Electoral Committee announces the commencement of nomination forms for interested candidates. Forms are available at the SUG Secretariat.
